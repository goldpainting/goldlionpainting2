# Frequently Asked Questions About Pressure Washing

## How often should homes be pressure washed in Florida?

Many Florida homes benefit from professional pressure washing every 1–2 years depending on moisture exposure, tree coverage, coastal conditions, and organic buildup.

## Is pressure washing important before exterior painting?

Yes. Pressure washing helps remove dirt, mold, mildew, chalking paint, and contaminants that can affect paint adhesion and coating durability.

## Can pressure washing damage stucco?

Improper pressure washing methods can damage some surfaces. Using the correct cleaning methods and pressure levels is important for protecting stucco and painted surfaces.

## What surfaces can be pressure washed?

Common surfaces include:
- Stucco
