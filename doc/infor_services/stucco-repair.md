# Frequently Asked Questions About Stucco Repair

## Are small stucco cracks normal in Florida homes?

Hairline stucco cracks are common in Florida due to heat, humidity, moisture exposure, and natural surface movement. However, proper repairs are important to help prevent moisture intrusion and paint failure.

## Does damaged stucco need to be repaired before painting?

Yes. Painting over damaged stucco without proper repairs can lead to continued cracking, bubbling, peeling, and premature coating failure.

## What causes stucco cracks in St. Petersburg homes?

Common causes include:
- Surface movement
- Moisture exposure
- Settlement
- Heat expansion and contraction
- Aging coatings
- Improper previous repairs

## What type of paint works best for stucco homes?

Premium exterior coatings and masonry primer systems designed for Florida conditions typically perform best on stucco homes.
